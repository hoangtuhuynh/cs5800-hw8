===== Part 1: Online and Cash on Delivery over Email and SMS =====

Online (credit card) + Email:
  [Email] To: alice@example.com | Subject: Payment update | Your credit card payment of $59.99 was received. Your order is being processed.

Online (PayPal) + SMS:
  [SMS] To: +1-555-0101 | Your PayPal payment of $24.50 was received. Your order is being processed.

Cash on delivery + Email:
  [Email] To: bob@example.com | Subject: Payment update | Your order is confirmed. Please have $35.00 in cash ready when the driver arrives.

Cash on delivery + SMS:
  [SMS] To: +1-555-0102 | Your order is confirmed. Please have $18.75 in cash ready when the driver arrives.
